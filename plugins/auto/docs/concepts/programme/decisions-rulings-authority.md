# Decisions, Rulings and Authority

A decision is logged with its reason. A Ruling is a dated or scoped order from Shawn. An Authority level says who decides a kind of choice (never, propose, act and tell, act). Some kinds are protected and always stay with Shawn.

- **Where it lives:** `lib/programme_decisions.py`, `lib/programme_authority.py`
- **Note:** The order of precedence is in `references/judgment.md`.
